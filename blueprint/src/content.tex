\bpintroheading

This blueprint follows the formalisation of characterisations of function spaces: the Besov
spaces $\Besov{s}{p}{q}$, the Triebel--Lizorkin spaces $\TL{s}{p}{q}$, the Sobolev spaces
$\Sob{m}{p}$ and the Bessel potential spaces $H^{s,p}$, each described in several ways, among
them by approximation, differences, Littlewood--Paley blocks, local means, atoms, wavelet
coefficients and weak derivatives, together with the proofs that the descriptions agree. The
settings are $\R^d$, the torus $\T^d$, intervals, the cube, Lipschitz domains and compact
manifolds.

The first part is the abstract theory of approximation spaces in the sense of
Pietsch~\cite{Pietsch}, which gives Besov spaces as approximation spaces and the embedding
theorems from a Nikolskii inequality; the second collects the tools from analysis. The Besov
part opens with an overview of the descriptions of Besov spaces and of the settings in which
each is proved. The parts on Triebel--Lizorkin spaces and on Sobolev and Bessel potential
spaces follow, and the last part carries the spaces to Lipschitz domains, their boundaries and
compact manifolds.

Each statement names the Lean declaration that formalises it. Every statement and every
proof is formalised: the project compiles without \texttt{sorry}, so each result is
verified by the Lean kernel.
